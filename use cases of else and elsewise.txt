1)Alternative Choices:
"I don't like coffee; what else do you have to drink?"

2)Sequential Ordering:
"First, we'll finish this task; then, we'll see what else needs to be done."

3)Request for Additional Information:
"Tell me about your hobbies. What else do you enjoy doing in your free time?"

4)Negation or Exclusion:
"I only have $10; I don't have anything else to give."

5)Inclusion:
"We invited Tom and Emily. Is there anyone else you'd like to invite?"

6)Time:
"I waited for an hour, but she didn't show up. What else could I do?"

'Elsewise':
 refers to an alternative or different way of doing something. 
example:I’ll try to fix the car, elsewise I’ll call a mechanic” means “I’ll try to fix the car myself, but if I can’t, I’ll call a mechanic instead.”
