1)Self-awareness:
 Identify what aspects of your current study patterns are not working for you.
 Understand your strengths and weaknesses, and be honest about what needs improvement.

2)Set clear goals:
 Define your study objectives and set specific, achievable goals.
 This helps provide direction and motivation.

3)Active learning:
 Engage in active learning methods, such as summarizing information in your own words, 
teaching concepts to someone else, or using mnemonic devices to aid memory.


4)Take breaks:
 Breaks are crucial for maintaining focus. Consider using techniques like the Pomodoro Technique,
 where you work for a set time and then take a short break.

5)Regular review: Schedule regular review sessions to reinforce what you've learned.
 Spaced repetition can be an effective technique for long-term retention.

6)Reward yourself: 
Celebrate your achievements, both big and small.
 Positive reinforcement can help reinforce new habits.

7)Mindfulness and stress management:
means staying focused on the present moment and finding ways to handle stress in a healthy way."
