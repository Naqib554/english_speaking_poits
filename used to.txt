"Used to" 

It's often used to talk about past habits, actions, or states that have changed over time. For example:
"I used to play soccer every Saturday when I was younger."
"She used to live in Paris before moving to London."
"They used to be best friends, but they drifted apart over the years."

The structure is usually "used to" + base form of the verb.. It's important to note that "used to"
indicates a past habit or state, while "would" can also be used for past habits but often implies a specific choice or willingness in the past.
