Make Sure:(یقینی بنائیں)


The phrase "make sure" is commonly used to emphasize the importance of verifying or confirming something. Here are some situations in which you might use "make sure":

Instructions or Directions: When giving instructions or directions, you might use "make sure" to emphasize the importance of following them accurately. For example: "Before you leave, make sure to turn off all the lights."

Checking Information: If you're asking someone to confirm information or details, you might use "make sure" to emphasize the need for accuracy. For example: "Can you make sure the report is ready by tomorrow?"

Safety Precautions: In situations where safety is important, you might use "make sure" to stress the importance of taking certain precautions. For example: "Make sure to wear your seatbelt."

Quality Control: In a professional or business context, you might use "make sure" to ensure that something meets certain standards or requirements. For example: "Please make sure that all documents are properly formatted before submitting them."

Overall, "make sure" is used to emphasize the necessity of verifying or confirming something, either for accuracy, safety, compliance, or quality assurance.
