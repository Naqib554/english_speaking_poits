Who: Used to inquire about a person or people.

Example: Who is at the door?
What: Asks about a thing, idea, or action.

Example: What is your favorite color?
Where: Queries about a place or location.

Example: Where did you go yesterday?
When: Asks about a specific time or period.

Example: When is your birthday?
Why: Inquires about the reason or purpose.

Example: Why are you smiling?
Which: Asks about a specific item from a limited set.

Example: Which book do you want?
Whose: Questions about possession or ownership.

Example: Whose car is parked outside?
Whom: Used to ask about the object of an action (less common in informal English).

Example: Whom did you invite to the party?
How: Inquires about the manner, condition, or method.

Example: How did you solve the problem?
How many: Asks about quantity or number.

Example: How many siblings do you have?
How much: Inquires about the amount, often related to quantity or cost.

Example: How much does it cost?



How often: Asks about the frequency or regularity of an action.

Example: How often do you go to the gym?
How long: Inquires about the duration of an activity or time.

Example: How long did the movie last?
How far: Asks about the distance to a place.

Example: How far is the airport from here?
How many times: Specifically inquires about the number of occurrences.

Example: How many times have you been to that restaurant?
How come: Used to ask about the reason or cause, similar to "why."

Example: How come you're not coming to the party?
What time: Asks about a specific time.

Example: What time is the meeting?
What kind of: Inquires about the type or category of something.

Example: What kind of music do you like?








What for: Asks about the purpose or reason.

Example: What are you buying this tool for?
What else: Inquires about additional or alternative things.

Example: What else do you need for the recipe?
What if: Used to pose a hypothetical situation or scenario.

Example: What if it rains during the outdoor event?
What about: Asks for information or opinions on a particular topic.

Example: What about your plans for the weekend?
How so: Seeks clarification on the manner or reason behind a statement.

Example: You're confident; how so?
In what way: Asks for details on the specific manner or method.

Example: In what way can I improve my performance?
For how long: Inquires about the duration of an activity.

Example: For how long have you been studying French?










What time does: Used to ask about the specific time of an event or activity.

Example: What time does the train leave?
What if not: Asks about the consequences or alternatives if a condition is not met.

Example: What if not everyone can attend the meeting?
How many more: Inquires about additional quantity or number.

Example: How many more chapters do we need to read?
To what extent: Asks about the degree or scope of something.

Example: To what extent do you agree with the statement?
In what way: Seeks information about the specific manner or method.

Example: In what way can I assist you?
