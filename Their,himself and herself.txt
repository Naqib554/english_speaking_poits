"Their"
It is used when talking about something that belongs to more than one person or
when the gender of the subject is unknown or unspecified.
For example, if we're talking about a group of friends, we might say
Example : "Their favorite game is soccer," 
Example:"Someone left their keys on the table."(suject is unknown in this case)
because it's the game that belongs to all of them together.



"Himself" 
It is used to refer to a single male person.
Example:"John cooked dinner for himself."
Example:"He only care about himself not others"


"Herself":
"Herself" is used to refer back to a female subject, emphasizing an action she does alone or for her own benefit
Example:"Sarah cooked dinner for herself."
Example:"She just does anything for herself"
Example"She always buys golden-colored gifts for herself on her birthday."


"Themselves"("خود")

Doing something to themselves:
"The children dressed themselves for school."
"The athletes pushed themselves to their limits during the competition."
Doing something for themselves:
"The friends organized the party themselves, without any help from adults."
"The employees decided to take a break and make themselves some coffee."
