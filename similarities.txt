For discussing similarities, we often use words like "similarly," "likewise," or "in the same way."


Similarly: Means in the same way.

Example: "She likes reading. Similarly, her friend enjoys books."
Likewise: Means also or in the same way.

Example: "He was helpful. Likewise, she offered help."
In the same way: Means exactly like.

Example: "She runs fast. In the same way, her brother is quick."
