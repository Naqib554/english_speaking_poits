
Use cases of all preposition:

In: Indicates location, time, or inclusion within a space.
On: Specifies a surface or a specific day/date.
At: Denotes a specific point in time or location.
By: Indicates a deadline or the means/method of achieving something.
With: Expresses association, possession, or accompaniment.
For: Indicates purpose, recipient, or duration.
Of: Denotes possession, origin, or material.
To: Indicates direction, destination, or purpose.
From: Denotes origin, source, or starting point.
Over: Indicates movement above or covering.
Under: Denotes position beneath or less than.
Between: Describes something in the middle of two things.
Among: Denotes being a part of a group or surrounded by others.
Through: Indicates movement from one side to another or completion.
Beside: Indicates a position next to or in comparison with.

Examples of all preposition:
In: I live in the city.
On: The cat is on the roof.
At: We'll meet at the restaurant.
By: I will finish the project by Monday.
With: She is walking with her friend.
For: This gift is for you.
Of: The pages of the book are yellow.
To: I'm going to the store.
From: I received a letter from my cousin.
Over: The bird flew over the trees.
Under: The keys are under the mat.
Between: The cat is between the two chairs.
Among: He is among the students in the class.
Through: We walked through the forest.
Beside: The flowers are beside the vase.
