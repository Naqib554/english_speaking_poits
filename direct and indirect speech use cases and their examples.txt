three rules for reported speech :Here is a **comprehensive lesson** on **Direct and Indirect Speech**:  

---

### **1. Definition**  
- **Direct Speech**: It involves quoting the exact words of the speaker, enclosed in quotation marks.  
  *Example*: Maria said, "I am happy today."  
- **Indirect Speech**: It involves reporting what someone said, without quoting their exact words.  
  *Example*: Maria said that she was happy that day.  

---

### **2. Why Do We Use It?**  
- To report someone's words indirectly, making them more concise and suitable for summarizing conversations or narrations.  
- It is widely used in storytelling, formal writing, and summarizing dialogues.

---

### **3. Key Changes in Direct to Indirect Speech**  
#### **A. Pronoun Changes**  
Pronouns are adjusted to reflect the perspective of the reporter:  
- **Direct**: John said, "I love my job."  
- **Indirect**: John said that he loved his job.  

| **Direct Pronoun** | **Indirect Pronoun** |
|--------------------|----------------------|
| I                 | he/she              |
| We                | they                |
| My                | his/her             |
| Our               | their               |
| You               | me/us               |

---

#### **B. Time and Place Changes**  
Words indicating time and place in direct speech often change:  

| **Direct Speech**       | **Indirect Speech**      |
|-------------------------|--------------------------|
| today                   | that day                |
| tomorrow                | the next day            |
| yesterday               | the previous day        |
| now                     | then                    |
| here                    | there                   |
| this                    | that                    |
| these                   | those                   |

*Example*:  
- Direct: She said, "I will visit you tomorrow."  
- Indirect: She said that she would visit me the next day.  

---

#### **C. Reporting Verbs**  
The main reporting verbs are:  
- *Said, told, asked, requested, ordered, exclaimed, explained, advised, promised*.  

---

### **4. Tense Backshifting Rules**  
Tenses in indirect speech generally shift one step back in time. Let’s review the changes for all **12 tenses**:  

| **Direct Speech Tense**          | **Indirect Speech Tense**       | **Example**                                              |
|----------------------------------|----------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| **Present Simple**               | Past Simple                     | "I like apples." → He said that he liked apples.         |
| **Present Continuous**           | Past Continuous                 | "I am working." → She said that she was working.         |
| **Present Perfect**              | Past Perfect                    | "I have finished." → He said that he had finished.       |
| **Present Perfect Continuous**   | Past Perfect Continuous         | "I have been studying." → She said she had been studying.|
| **Past Simple**                  | Past Perfect                    | "I ate lunch." → He said he had eaten lunch.             |
| **Past Continuous**              | Past Perfect Continuous         | "I was reading." → She said she had been reading.        |
| **Past Perfect**                 | No Change                       | "I had slept." → He said he had slept.                  |
| **Past Perfect Continuous**      | No Change                       | "I had been walking." → She said she had been walking.   |
| **Future Simple (will)**         | Would                           | "I will go." → He said he would go.                     |
| **Future Continuous**            | Would Be                        | "I will be working." → She said she would be working.    |
| **Future Perfect**               | Would Have                      | "I will have finished." → He said he would have finished.|
| **Future Perfect Continuous**    | Would Have Been                 | "I will have been studying." → She said she would have been studying.|

---

### **5. Examples for Each Case**  
#### 1. **Present Simple → Past Simple**  
- Direct: "I play football."  
- Indirect: He said that he played football.  

#### 2. **Present Continuous → Past Continuous**  
- Direct: "I am writing a book."  
- Indirect: She said that she was writing a book.  

#### 3. **Present Perfect → Past Perfect**  
- Direct: "I have completed my homework."  
- Indirect: He said that he had completed his homework.  

#### 4. **Past Simple → Past Perfect**  
- Direct: "I visited Paris last year."  
- Indirect: She said that she had visited Paris the previous year.  

#### 5. **Future Simple → Would**  
- Direct: "I will help you."  
- Indirect: He said that he would help me.
