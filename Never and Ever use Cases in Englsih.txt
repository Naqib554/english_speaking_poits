"Ever" and "never" are both adverbs related to time, but they have opposite meanings.

Ever means "at any time." It is used in affirmative sentences, questions, and conditional sentences.
Example: "Have you ever been to Paris?"

Never means "at no time" or "not ever." It is used in negative sentences to indicate that something has not happened at any time.

Example: "I have never been to Paris."
