Deal and Handle:


Deal:(Use "deal" for managing challenges in english communication)
Example_1:"She had to deal with a difficult customer who was unhappy with the service."
Example_2:"I had to deal with a lot of stress during the exam period."
Example_3:"I had to deal with difficult situations during my BSCS education journey."


Handle:(Use "handle" for managing tasks or responsibilities in english communication)
Example_1:"He handles all the financial matters for the company."
Example_2:"Can you handle organizing the event next week?"
Example_3:"can you handle the design of final year project"



Note:  "While both words can sometimes be used interchangeably,but in some cases, 
one word may be more suitable or idiomatic than the other."


slight difference  and subtle difference:

1)"slight difference":
This phrase is used when the difference between two things is small or minor
but not necessarily difficult to perceive.
Example_1: The temperature difference between two cups of coffee, one slightly warmer than the other.(It doen't require deeper understanding)
Example_2: The taste of two brands of chocolate, with one having a slightly richer flavor.(It doen't require deeper understanding)
Example_1: The size discrepancy between two shoes, one being marginally larger than the other.(It doen't require deeper understanding)


2)"subtle difference":
This phrase is used when the  difference between tow things is very small that is not immediately obvious but can be discerned with careful attention.
Example: "There is a subtle difference between 'Handle' and 'Deal' "(It requires deeper understanding)
