
instead of "to whome" we use "to whcih" in english for non living things.

 "To which" is used to refer to a non-living thing in a formal way. It connects a sentence to something previously mentioned.

Examples:
"This is the book to which I was referring."
(Refers to "book" in a formal way)

"The rule to which you must follow is strict."
(Refers to "rule")

It works like "which... to", but in a formal structure.
