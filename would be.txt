"Would be"
“Would have” is followed by the past participle when you make an unreal past conditional sentence:
I would have helped you if you had asked me.
They would have come if we had invited them.


1)Hypothetical Scenarios:
When discussing unreal or hypothetical situations, use “would be.” For example:
“If I were rich, I would be traveling the world.”
“She said the concert would be amazing.”

 "Would" is also used as a replacement for "will" in reported speech. For example:

Direct Speech: He said, "I will kill you."
