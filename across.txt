
Use "across" 
when talking about going from one side to another, comparing things, or spanning a distance.
Physical Movement: "She walked across the street." This implies movement from one side of the street to the other.
Geographical Locations: "They traveled across Europe." This suggests movement through various parts of Europe.
Spacial Relationships: "The book was placed across the table." This indicates the book's position from one side to another on the table.
Comparisons: "Across the world, people celebrate New Year's Eve differently." This means people in different parts of the world celebrate New Year's Eve in diverse ways.
Distribution or Extent: "There are shops scattered across the city." This expresses the idea of shops being spread throughout the city.
