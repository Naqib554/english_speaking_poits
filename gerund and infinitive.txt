Gerunds and Infinitives:
 Gerunds and infinitives are both forms of verbs that can function as nouns in a sentence,
 but they are used in different ways.

Formed by adding "-ing" to the base form of a verb.
Used as nouns and can function as subjects, objects, or objects of prepositions.
Example: "Swimming is a great form of exercise."
Infinitives:

The base form of a verb preceded by the word "to" (e.g., to swim, to read).
Also used as nouns and can function as subjects, objects, or objects of prepositions.
Example: "To swim every day is my goal."
