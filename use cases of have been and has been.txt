Completed Actions with Present Relevance:
I have been to the beach. (I went to the beach at some point, and it matters now.)
He has been to that restaurant before. (The action of going to the restaurant is completed, and there's a connection to the present.)

Experiences and Accomplishments:
She has been an excellent student throughout her academic career. (Indicating a state or condition achieved over a period of time.)

Present Result of Past Actions:
The car has been repaired. (The action of repairing the car is completed, and the car is now in a repaired state.)

Continuous Actions that Started in the Past:
She has been working hard. (She started working hard in the past, and she still is.)
She has been working on the project for months. (Indicates an action that started in the past and continues into the present.)
