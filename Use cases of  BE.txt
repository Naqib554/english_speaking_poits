"Being" is the present participle form of the verb "to be." 
It is used in continuous tenses (present continuous, past continuous, future continuous) 
to indicate an ongoing action or state.
Example: "She is being very helpful today." (present continuous)
Example: "They were being noisy during the movie." (past continuous)
Example: "He will be being interviewed tomorrow." (future continuous)
