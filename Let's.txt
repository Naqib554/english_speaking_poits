Let's  is the contraction of "let us."
Informal: “Okay. We’re all ready. Let’s go.”
Formal: “Ladies and gentlemen, let us remember all those who have contributed to this project.”

Since you plan to check the equipment after reaching the lab, this tense is suitable instead of using future tense 
and you can say the bellow statement confidently:
Example:“Let’s go to the lab and check all the equipment of the computer system.”
