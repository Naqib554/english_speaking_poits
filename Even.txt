even:
1) To show balance.
example: we are even.

2)to show that something is extreme or surprising.
example: we don't have even money.
example:He didn't even say goodbye.
example:"We don't even have time to catch our breath."
example: I don't even have enough money to buy the pen.(It effectively communicates the idea that the speaker
 lacks enough money to purchase the pen.)

Yes, in simple terms, the speaker is telling the 
listener that they are surprised or shocked because they don't have enough money to buy a pen, 
and they're sharing this surprise with the listener.
