How: Asks about the way or method.

Example: How do you ride a bike?
How many: Asks about the number of countable things.

Example: How many cookies do you have?
How much: Asks about the amount of something uncountable.

Example: How much sugar is in the bowl?
How many times: Asks how often something happens.

Example: How many times have you been to the beach?
How far: Asks about the distance between places.

Example: How far is the park from here?
How long: Asks about the duration of time.

Example: How long is the movie?
As: Shows similarity or equality.
Example: She is as fast as a cheetah.

As many: Shows equality in countable quantity.
Example: I have as many toys as you.

As much: Shows equality in quantity for uncountable things.
Example: Can I have as much ice cream as you?

As many times: Shows equality in frequency.
Example: I've called you as many times as you called me.

As far: Shows equality in distance.
Example: The store is not as far as I thought.

As long: Shows equality in duration.
Example: Can you stay as long as you want?
