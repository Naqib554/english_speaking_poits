Ago and Before:
there is a slight difference between the word ago and before:
So, "ago" is often used to specify a precise duration in the past from now, while 
"before" is used to establish a temporal relationship between two events.

"Ago" is used to describe how long ago something happened from the present moment, like "three days ago."
"She left the party two hours ago."
"I completed my homework twenty minutes ago."
Example : "he called me 2 days ago"


"Before" indicates an earlier point in time relative to another event or point of reference, like "before the meeting."
Example: "I finished my work before the deadline."
Example: "He called me before he went to bed."
Example: "I met him before joining the meeting"
