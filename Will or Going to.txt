"Will" or "Going to"? 

Today, we're looking at how to correctly use 'will' and 'going to' for talking about the future. Let's explore these two forms:


Will : used for making predictions, promises, offers, and decisions made at the moment of speaking

Examples: 

I think it will rain tomorrow. (Prediction without evidence)

I will help you with that. (Offer)

It will not happen again. (Promise)




Going to : used for planned actions or when there is evidence something is likely to happen.



Examples:

Look at those clouds! It's going to rain soon. (Prediction based on evidence)

I am going to study medicine next year. (Planned action)
