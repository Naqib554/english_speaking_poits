The sentence "God just might be answering that person's prayer through you" means that God could be helping someone by using you.



Here’s a breakdown with simple words:
"God" - The one who helps.
"just might" - It is possible.
"be answering" - Helping or giving what was asked.
"that person's prayer" - What someone is asking from God.
"through you" - Using you to help.
So, it means God could be using you to help someone else.
