who and whom:

who=subjective pronoun
whom= objective pronoun

note: 'who' use in informal conversation instead of "whom"

example_1: My friend who love me.(is sentence ma 'love' mera friend krta hain muj se)
example_2: my friend whom I love .(is sentence ma 'love' ma krta houn apna friend se)
