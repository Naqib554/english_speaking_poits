Linking verb:
A linking verb is a type of verb that connects the subject of a sentence to additional information about the subject.
Unlike action verbs (which describe actions), linking verbs do not show an action; instead, they express a state of being.
Linkin_verb:They merely indicate existence or the state of the subject.

Examples of linking verbs include “To Be,” “become,” “seem,” “appear,” “look,” “feel,” “smell,” “sound,” and “taste.”
