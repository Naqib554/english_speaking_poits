Here are the use cases for "could have been" and "should have been":

Could have been – Used for possibilities in the past (things that were possible but didn't happen).

Example: He could have been a great doctor, but he chose another career.
Meaning: It was possible for him to become a doctor, but it didn’t happen.
Should have been – Used for expectations or advice in the past (things that were expected but didn't happen).

Example: You should have been more careful with that glass.
Meaning: It was expected or advised that you be careful, but you weren't.
Both phrases express regret, advice, or unrealized possibilities in the past.
