subordinate and unsubordinate cluase:

subordinate Clause:(A dependent sentence).
unsubordinate Clause:(Independent clause,A standalone, complete sentence).

In English grammar,
it's common to use the present tense in subordinate clauses that refer to future events.
This is because the subordinate clause depends on the main clause, which is in the future tense.
The present tense in the subordinate clause shows that the action or state will happen at the same time or after the main clause. For example:
Example_1:This time, he will be well when he meets us.
