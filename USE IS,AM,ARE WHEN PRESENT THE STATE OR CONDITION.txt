So, use "am/is/are" when describing a state or condition, not an action.


Here are 10 examples of using am/is/are to describe a state or condition (not an action):

I am proud of my achievements.
She is tired after a long day.
They are excited about the trip.
The sky is clear and blue today.
He is responsible for the project.
The food is delicious and fresh.
We are happy to see you again.
The room is quiet and peaceful.
This book is very interesting to read.
I am grateful for your help.
