Negative contractions :
I'm (I am): میں ہوں (Main hoon) - Negative: I'm not (I am not)
You're (You are): تم ہو (Tum ho) - Negative: You're not (You are not)
He's (He is): وہ ہے (Woh hai) - Negative: He's not (He is not)
She's (She is): وہ ہے (Woh hai) - Negative: She's not (She is not)
It's (It is): یہ ہے (Yeh hai) - Negative: It's not (It is not)
We're (We are): ہم ہیں (Hum hain) - Negative: We're not (We are not)
They're (They are): وہ ہیں (Woh hain) - Negative: They're not (They are not)
Can't (Cannot): نہیں ہو سکتا (Nahi ho sakta) - Negative: Can't (Cannot) be contracted negatively.
Don't (Do not): نہیں کرتا (Nahi karta) - Negative: Doesn't (Does not)
Won't (Will not): نہیں کرے گا (Nahi karega) - Negative: Won't (Will not) be contracted negatively.
I've (I have): میرے پاس ہے (Mere paas hai) - Negative: I haven't (I have not)
You've (You have): تمہارے پاس ہے (Tumhare paas hai) - Negative: You haven't (You have not)
Shouldn't (Should not): نہیں چاہئے (Nahi chahiye) - Negative: Shouldn't (Should not)
Couldn't (Could not): نہیں ہو سکا (Nahi ho sakaa) - Negative: Couldn't (Could not)
Haven't (Have not): نہیں ہے (Nahi hai) - Negative: Haven't (Have not) be contracted negatively.
I'll (I will): میں کرے گا (Main karega) - Negative: I won't (I will not)
You'll (You will): تم کرو گے (Tum kro ge) - Negative: You won't (You will not)
He'll (He will): وہ کرے گا (Woh karega) - Negative: He won't (He will not)
She'll (She will): وہ کرے گی (Woh karegi) - Negative: She won't (She will not)
We'll (We will): ہم کریں گے (Hum karenge) - Negative: We won't (We will not)
